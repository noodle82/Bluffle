Contact us

Email support@bluffle.co.uk

For gameplay help, bug reports, accessibility feedback or privacy questions, use the address above. If the button does not open an email app, copy the address into your email service.

Include your device model, operating system or browser version, the BLUFFLE! version if shown, and the steps that led to the problem. A short description is often enough.

Please remove personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords or payment-card details. Younger players can ask a trusted adult for help, and can also contact us with their own privacy questions.

What works today?

Current version

Local gameplay

The current game is played locally on your device. There is no online multiplayer or cloud game syncing.

£3.99 Premium is planned. Purchasing and restoring purchases have not yet been implemented. These are future features, not services you can use today.

Common questions

The game has stopped responding. What can I try?

First, note what happened and save a screenshot if useful. Close and reopen the game, or refresh its browser tab. This may interrupt the current round. Check for an available update. If it happens again, email us the steps that caused it.

Will clearing data fix a problem?

It may reset the game, but it may also remove locally saved information and settings. Contact us first if you want to preserve anything. We cannot recover local game data remotely.

Where are Premium and Restore Purchases?

They are planned features and do not work in the current version. The planned Premium price is £3.99. We will provide purchase and restoration instructions when those features are implemented. Do not send money or payment details to support.

I think I was charged. What should I do?

The current version has no working purchase flow. Check the seller and app name on your receipt with your app store or payment provider. If it appears to concern BLUFFLE!, email us a description with personal and payment details removed. Do not buy again to resolve the issue.

Can you delete information about me?

We can help with personal information held in support correspondence. Information held only on your device must be removed using that device’s controls. Email us for help, and read the Privacy Policy for your choices and rights.
