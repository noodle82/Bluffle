The current game

Available today: local play

BLUFFLE! is a family bluffing game played locally on a device. The current version does not provide online multiplayer or cloud game syncing.

Premium purchasing and restoring purchases are not implemented. A price label or a preview of a future feature does not make it available to buy.

These terms describe the current game and these information pages. Read them before playing. If anything is unclear, contact support@bluffle.co.uk.

Playing together

Use BLUFFLE! for personal, non-commercial play. If you are a child, ask a parent or trusted adult to help you understand these terms. Adults should help choose suitable play and supervise as needed.

Bluffing belongs inside the game. Treat other players kindly, stop if someone is uncomfortable, and avoid entering private or hurtful information. Do not use the game unlawfully or deliberately disrupt it.

Keep the device safe when sharing it. Other players can see information displayed on it. Keep a copy of anything important before clearing site or app data.
