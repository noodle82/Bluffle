For younger players

Your privacy, in simple words

BLUFFLE! is played locally on your device. Your game choices are not sent to us as an online game.

Use a nickname instead of your full name. Don’t put your address, school, phone number or other private information into a game or screenshot.

If you email us, we can see your email address and what you send. You can ask a trusted adult to help, but you can also ask us privacy questions yourself.

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We’ll explain what we can do in words you can understand.

Information during play

The current game runs locally. Information entered for a game, such as a player nickname or a game choice, is handled on the device rather than sent to us for online gameplay. We do not operate online multiplayer or cloud game syncing in this version.

Other people using or looking at the device may see the game. Device backups and browser or operating-system settings may also affect locally held data.

To remove locally saved information, use your browser’s site-data controls or your device’s app-data controls, as appropriate to the version you use. This may reset progress and settings. We cannot retrieve or delete a local game remotely. Contact us if you need help before clearing anything.

Visiting these pages

These three pages do not set cookies, read local storage, use analytics, display advertising or load third-party fonts or scripts.

Hosting still involves technical data. When these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its handling of hosting data, including retention and international transfers, is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from local gameplay.

Links to other websites open services with their own privacy practices. This statement about these three pages does not describe any separate game page, app-store service or email service.

If you contact support

If you email support@bluffle.co.uk, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate a problem and keep necessary records of how it was resolved. You do not need to email us to play.

Please send only the information needed for your question. Remove names and other personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords, payment-card details or information about children that we do not need.

Our proposed lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing requested support and protecting the game and its users. Where a record is required by law, we rely on that legal obligation. We do not use support messages for marketing.

Support information is handled by the people dealing with your request and the email provider used to receive and store it. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. We keep it only while needed to resolve the issue, deal with follow-up questions or meet a legal requirement, then delete or anonymise it.

Confirmation required: the owner must confirm the support lawful basis, email provider, access arrangements, retention criteria and any overseas processing and safeguards before this notice is published.

Premium is planned, not available

A Premium option at a planned price of £3.99 and a restore-purchases feature are planned. Neither purchasing nor restoring purchases has been implemented in the current version.

There is currently no working BLUFFLE! purchase or restore flow. This policy does not describe an active payment integration. Before purchases launch, we will explain the actual store/payment provider, purchase information processed, receipt checks, retention and relevant rights in an updated notice.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for access to personal information we hold, correction, deletion or restriction of its use. Portability may apply in certain circumstances. These rights have legal limits, and some depend on how the information is used.

Your right to object: you can object to use based on legitimate interests by emailing support@bluffle.co.uk. We will consider your circumstances and explain our response.

If processing relies on consent in future, you will be able to withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful use. The current support basis proposed above is legitimate interests, not consent.

Children have privacy rights too. We can explain them directly to a child and support an appropriate representative. We may ask for proportionate information to make sure we are responding to the right person.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). You do not have to contact us first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when the game or our handling of information changes, including before purchases or new online features launch. The review date above identifies this draft; it is not a statement that it has been published.
